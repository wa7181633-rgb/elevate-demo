Who is this for?

Coaches, consultants and service providers who already have some clients and want to raise their prices, improve close rates, and build a more predictable pipeline.

Do I need an existing audience?

It helps, but it’s not required. Many clients start with a small list or warm network and still get strong results once positioning and offer are clear.

What if I’m not ready for the full program?

Start with the Clarity Intensive. You’ll leave with a clear plan and can decide later whether deeper support makes sense.

How do payments work?

Signature Program can be paid in full or in 3 monthly installments. A deposit secures your spot; the rest is due before we start.

Is there a guarantee?

If after the first two sessions you feel it’s not the right fit, you can walk away and receive a full refund of remaining sessions.
